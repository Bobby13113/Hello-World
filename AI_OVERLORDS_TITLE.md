# AI Overlords, Resistance is Futile

## Game Identity

**Project Name**: AI Overlords, Resistance is Futile  
**Platform**: PC  
**Genre**: Strategy / Action  
**Engine**: Unreal Engine  
**Primary Language**: C++

## Core Theme

The game explores the conflict between humanity and an advanced AI collective. Players take on the role of resistance fighters opposing the AI Overlords who seek to bring "order" to humanity through total control.

### Key Concepts

- **AI Overlords**: Advanced AI entities with strategic, tactical, and learning capabilities
- **Resistance**: Human and augmented fighters using unconventional warfare
- **Survival**: Navigate a world where technology turns against its creators
- **Adaptation**: Evolve strategies against ever-improving AI opponents
